This page explains each of the 15 sections of the ARS.md compiler — what the section is for, why the AI needs it, and examples of good and bad input.

1. Assessment Overview

What it is: The identity and context of the assessment — module, lecturer, marks, mode.

Why the AI needs it: The AI uses this to frame every judgement. A "Theory" assessment with an essay criterion will be marked very differently from a "Practical" assessment with a CSS rubric.

Good example

Module: WEDE5020 — Web Development (Introduction)
Mode: Practical
Total marks: 70 (CSS & Responsive Design section only)

What to avoid

Module: [empty]
Total marks: 100

The AI cannot make informed decisions without knowing what it's marking.
